---
title: Before choosing a treatment, define the question.
description: A practical checklist for preparing for an aesthetic medicine consultation in Seoul.
category: Care · Perspective
---

A treatment name can make a question feel settled before the conversation has begun. A more useful starting point is to describe what you notice, when it bothers you, and what you hope would change.

## Describe the concern in your own words

Bring your questions to the consultation. If you have had treatment before, explain what was done, approximately when, and what you liked or did not like about the experience. Your physician can ask for the detail needed to assess your options.

## Make room for alternatives

Ask what else could address the concern, what may be left unchanged, and what waiting would mean. You do not need to arrive committed to a device, product or combination.

## Make the practical details explicit

Your visit has a timeline beyond the clinic appointment. Tell the clinic about your departure date, important events and ability to return for follow-up. Ask about expected recovery, possible complications, the written aftercare and the full quote before agreeing to treatment.

## Leave with a clear next step

A useful consultation can end with treatment, a staged plan, more questions, or a decision to wait. What matters is understanding what has been proposed and why.
