I’m writing by the pool at Anantara Riverside in Bangkok, in the strong tropical sun my patients ask about when they book a laser before a holiday. After a pigment laser, protect the treated skin from sunlight as it heals, then keep up daily sun protection if melasma is a concern. Inflammation can leave a fresh brown mark, and light can darken melasma again. [1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24320057/) [4](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/a-z/melasma-self-care)

## Why would a spot look darker after a laser meant to lighten it?

Inflammation from treatment can leave extra pigment where the skin was injured. The medical term is post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, or PIH. A flat brown mark left behind by a healed pimple is a familiar example of the same response.

In a small trial of fractional laser resurfacing, researchers treated both sides of each face. The side that received sunscreen with anti-inflammatory ingredients as well as ointment showed less pigment at the first-week check than the side that received ointment alone. That finding is specific to the procedure and the formula tested. If a treated spot changes colour, ask the treating physician to look at it before deciding what it is. [1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24320057/)

## Can melasma come back even after the patches fade?

Yes. Melasma can become visible again with sun exposure, even after the treated skin looks even. PIH can form at a point of injury. With melasma, the familiar patch on your cheek may darken again long after the skin has healed. That is why daily sun protection still makes sense when there is very little pigment left to see. [2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25443629/) [4](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/a-z/melasma-self-care)

## Does sunlight matter if my skin never turns red?

Yes. Both long-wave ultraviolet A (UVA) and visible light, the part of sunlight we can see, can deepen pigmentation. In a controlled study, visible light produced lasting colour in volunteers with darker skin. Redness is a poor way to judge how much light your patches have received. [3](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20410914/)

## Which sunscreen should I buy for melasma?

Look for a broad-spectrum, water-resistant sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher. For melasma, choose a tint that contains iron oxides and matches your skin closely enough that you will wear a full layer. Broad spectrum means protection against UVA and UVB. Iron oxides help screen visible light as well. [4](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/a-z/melasma-self-care) [5](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/sun-protection/shade-clothing-sunscreen/how-to-apply-sunscreen)

In a randomized summer study, melasma worsened less with a sunscreen that also screened shorter wavelengths of visible light. The study followed people with melasma. It cannot tell us how much a tint will help an individual laser-treated freckle. [2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25443629/)

## Can I put sunscreen on the treated spot straight away?

Ask the physician who did the procedure when to start on that spot. If the surface is open or covered by a dressing, follow the wound-care instructions and keep it out of direct sun. The early-use study tested a particular preparation under supervision. It gives me no basis to tell you to rub your usual tinted sunscreen onto raw skin. [1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24320057/)

## How much should I use, and when do I put it on again?

Once the treated skin is ready for sunscreen, the American Academy of Dermatology recommends at least a teaspoon for the face. Apply it about 15 minutes before going outdoors. Cover exposed ears and neck too. [5](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/sun-protection/shade-clothing-sunscreen/how-to-apply-sunscreen)

Outdoors, reapply at least every two hours, and immediately after swimming or sweating. That last instruction is easy to miss on holiday. Once your physician has cleared you to swim, keep the tube beside your towel so it is there when you get out. [4](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/a-z/melasma-self-care) [5](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/sun-protection/shade-clothing-sunscreen/how-to-apply-sunscreen)

## Is a pool umbrella enough if I also wear a hat?

Use the umbrella and a wide-brimmed hat, and use sunscreen on exposed, healed skin as well. Look at the chair by the pool: its back may be in shade while the sun still reaches your cheek. Move the chair as the shade shifts, or take lunch indoors. A brim that shades your cheeks will do more for them than a cap that leaves them in the sun. [4](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/a-z/melasma-self-care)

## Should I wait until after my tropical holiday to have the laser?

If the holiday is built around swimming or long days outside, I would discuss doing the laser after you return. I found no clinical trial that sets a safe interval between every kind of pigment laser and a tropical pool holiday. A city trip with lunch indoors leaves different choices from a day on a boat. Before you settle on a treatment date, show your physician the day on your itinerary when shade will be hardest to find.
